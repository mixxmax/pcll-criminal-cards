## 0C. 每条理由的段落模式

讲评给出的固定四步：找出事实与法律问题 → 陈述法律原则 → 把事实适用于法律 → 下结论定级〔R21 L106 "issue spot the facts and law,"〕〔R21 L107 "state the legal principle,"〕〔R21 L108 "apply the facts to the law,"〕。

| 步 | 内容 | 要求 | 依据 |
|---|---|---|---|
| ① 标题 | 用"谁做错了什么"写成一句，如"(2) The TJ shifted the burden of proof" | 标题本身就是理由 | 〔L12 L102 "(2)The TJ shifted the burden of proof"〕；讲评标题同样以错误行为命名〔📄R24D Slide 6〕 |
| ② 原则 | 规则＋判例名或条文 | 判例写名称即可，不必完整出处 | 〔R22 L275 "The case name is sufficient"〕；须引判例和条文〔R21 L246 "you must cite cases, sections of Ordinances and apply the facts to the law"〕 |
| ③ 适用 | 引题面原话、指出具体错误及对被告的影响 | 讲评反复批评分析不足 | 〔R22 L184 "Again many students lost marks for insufficient analysis of facts and law, insufficient citation of cases."〕 |
| ④ 定级 | strong／good／reasonable／weak／no ground，必要时说明为何不是更强 | 必须给结论 | 〔R22 L250 "Must advise on strength of grounds you identify"〕 |

**范例段（LGS12，逐字照录）**：

> 〔L12 L104 "The TJ has misapplied the doctrine of recent possession."〕原文：*"The TJ has misapplied the doctrine of recent possession. Whilst the doctrine allows the judge to reject the implausible evidence of a defendant found in possession of stolen goods it does not shift the burden onto the defendant to prove his innocence: Chan Ying Kit v. R CACC 121/1980. In her reasons for verdict the TJ stated that as the client could not prove he was returning the stolen goods to the police the doctrine of recent possession alone was enough to convict him. This is a strong ground of appeal."*

拆解：第一句点出错误；第二句原则＋判例；第三句引裁决理由原话作适用；第四句定级。这与 EX01 §1 前的通用 IRAC 插槽（I 点出错误 → R 规则 → A 事实与影响 → C 归类与定级）及本卡 §0G 插口规格是同一顺序。

## 0D. 定级措辞与依据

> 各情形"怎么写才拿分"的英文例句（含所需判例与测试）见 EX01 §0A 定级规律表；本节只列定级用语。

| 定级 | 何时用 | 往届例子 |
|---|---|---|
| strong／very strong | 错误法律测试、强制程序未遵守、明显不可采证据进入且影响大 | 〔📄R1419 Slide 31〕〔📄R24S Slide 7〕 |
| good | 规则清楚、适用成立但影响程度可争 | 〔R21 L136 "Conclusion: good ground of appeal"〕 |
| reasonable（borderline） | 依赖可信度、律师失职门槛、酌情决定 | 〔R21 L167 "Conclusion – weaker ground of appeal than other grounds but reasonable in circumstances."〕 |
| weak | 只凭可信度、律师战术选择 | 〔📄R24D Slide 10〕 |
| no ground | 法律允许的做法、刑期合理或偏轻 | 〔📄R1419 Slide 27〕〔📄R24S Slide 10〕 |

教授在 SG08 的口头分级也用类似说法（"okay ground… not the strongest"）〔SG8T L94 "I think it's it's an okay ground. It's not the strongest."〕。

## 0F. 教授与讲评口径（应当／不应当）

| 应当 | 依据 |
|---|---|
| 尽量多写；拿不准的理由也写，放后面 | 〔R22 L279 "As much as you possibly can!"〕〔R21 L247 "include it anyway. You will not lose marks for irrelevant grounds"〕 |
| 理由太少时回头重读题面 | 〔R21 L245 "If you haven’t got many grounds of appeal – go back, reread the fact pattern"〕 |
| 题面附的法例、附表一定有用 | 〔R21 L226 "Many students missed this even though the Schedule was included in your exam paper!"〕 |
| 讼费一定会考 | 〔R22 L183 "Surprisingly many students did not do well on the costs section."〕 |
| 量刑上诉与否都要有理由；理由合理即可得分 | 〔R22 L187 "marks were allowed for sensible discussion and analysis of issues even if ultimately they concluded sentence was not manifestly excessive"〕 |
| 利用 RPE 的研究：控罪的可能刑期、上诉法院 | 〔R22 L238 "Your research on the charge(s) should have identified the likely sentence and the court to appeal to."〕 |
| 被告清白记录要进入承认事实或证据 | 〔SG8T L498 "make sure you get them into the admitted facts"〕 |
| 保释等候上诉先看胜算 | 〔SG8T L274 "whether or not the client has a strong case for appeal"〕 |

| 不应当 | 依据 |
|---|---|
| 只写结论不写原则和判例 | 〔R21 L246 "Most students did not include enough detail in their answers"〕 |
| 漏掉陪审团引导错误、相似事实 | 〔R22 L185 "Many students missed the wrong directions to the jury."〕 |
| 编造题面没有的非理由 | 〔R22 L250 "stick to those clearly identified by the fact pattern rather than any possible non-ground that you can think of!"〕 |
| 只上诉其中一项刑期而不顾其余 | 〔R22 L148 "appealing one sentence means appealing them all"〕 |

## 0G. 可插拔模板（固定部分＋插口）

> **官方有无模板**：本届没有空白的备忘录模板文件；官方给的是 LGS12 的 To Chi Wing 范例备忘录〔L12 L32 "We will examine the following model Memorandum of Advice"〕。本模板的固定部分取自该范例的结构，插口按 EX01 的格式设计。考试只交这一份备忘录（Q1 的 Notice 已在考前上传）〔MEL L30 "You have already uploaded your Notice of Objection onto Moodle."〕。
> **用法**：固定部分照写、填方括号；遇到 EX01 里的事实类型，就把那一行按"插口规格"改写成一段，插进对应插口。

### 一、插口规格：EX01 一行怎样变成备忘录一段

| 备忘录里的位置 | 从 EX01 取哪一列 | 改写要求 |
|---|---|---|
| 理由标题 | "事实类型"＋"错误方式" | 改成"谁做错了什么"，例如"The Judge reversed the burden of proof"〔L12 L102 "(2)The TJ shifted the burden of proof"〕 |
| 第一句：点出错误 | "事实类型"＋"错误方式" | 一句话说明谁做错了什么（对应 EX01 插槽的 I 句） |
| 第二句：法律原则 | "规则（考场可写）" | 原样写出，保留判例名和条文；判例写名称即可〔R22 L275 "The case name is sufficient"〕 |
| 第三句起：适用 | 题面事实＋"应用要点（对辩方的意义）" | 引题面原话，指出具体错误；再按该行"应用要点"说明对被告或辩方的影响——讲评扣分最多处 |
| 影响与但书 | "上诉落点"（归类／倾向、但书） | 写明为何影响结果；unsafe 类写"the proviso cannot apply" |
| 结论句 | "上诉落点"的定级 | *"This is a [strong / good / reasonable / weak] ground of appeal."* |
| 录音补充、⚠️ 标注 | 该行的补充 | 用来决定写多详细、是否放进非理由 |

> 插口规格与 EX01 §1 前的"通用 IRAC 插槽"是同一结构：I 一句错误 → R 规则 → A 事实与影响 → C 归类、但书与定级。

### 二、模板

```text
MEMO

Date: [date of exam]
From: Trainee Solicitor
To: Supervising Partner
Re: HKSAR v [Defendant] – grounds of appeal against conviction,
 sentence and costs

1. Introduction
This memo advises whether [D] has any arguable grounds of appeal
against his conviction on [date] in the [Court of First Instance /
District Court / Magistrates' Court] of [offence], the sentence of
[term] imposed on him and the order that he pay the Prosecution's
costs, and on the steps to be taken to protect his interests.

2. Summary of advice
In my opinion, [D] should appeal to the [Court of Appeal / Court of
First Instance] against [conviction / sentence / the costs order].
The strongest grounds are [ground 1] and [ground 2]. [D should / should
not] appeal against sentence because [reason]. [D should apply for
bail pending appeal.]

PART 1

3. Grounds of appeal against conviction
 ▶ 插口 A（可重复，按强弱排序，强的在前）
 取材：EX01 §1–§6 任一行；重复 Notice 的那条按 §0A "四要素"写
(1) [Heading: who did what wrong]
[Rule column.] [Facts from the paper, quoting the words used, and the
effect on D.] [Effect on the verdict / proviso.] This is a [grade]
ground of appeal.
(2) ...

4. Sentence
 ▶ 插口 B　取材：EX01 §7（G 组）＋ §10A 表二"是否上诉量刑"
[Starting point and tariff authority.] [Aggravating / mitigating
factors and any error of principle.] [Totality / consecutive.]
[Risk of increase under s. 83I CPO or loss of time under s. 83W CPO.]
Conclusion: [D should / should not] appeal against sentence.

5. Costs
 ▶ 插口 C　取材：EX01 §8（H 组）
Costs do not follow the event in criminal proceedings. Although a
convicted defendant is liable for costs under s. 12 CCCO, an order
should only be made in exceptional circumstances, e.g. where he
brought suspicion on himself or unnecessarily prolonged the
proceedings. Costs are compensatory, not punitive (s. 15 CCCO), and the
defendant's means must be considered: HKSAR v Wai Sau Cheong. Here
[facts]. This is a [grade] ground of appeal against costs.

6. Matters which are not grounds of appeal
```
